Privacy Policy

Archery Target

Archery Target is a simple single-player VR archery simulation for Meta Quest VR headsets and controllers. This policy explains what information is handled by the game and what is not collected by Caffeine Studios.

Last updated: May 11, 2026

Quick Summary

  • No account is required.
  • No multiplayer features are included.
  • No microphone, voice chat, or camera access is used.
  • No personal data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • The optional leaderboard name is stored locally in the game.

1. Who We Are

Archery Target is developed and published by Caffeine Studios. The game is a VR archery simulation with three play modes: Time Mode, Perfect Ten, and Infinity Mode.

2. Information We Collect

Caffeine Studios does not collect personal information through Archery Target. The game does not require an account, email address, phone number, profile, or online login to play.

Archery Target includes an in-game local leaderboard. Players may optionally enter a name for display on that leaderboard. This name is used only inside the game leaderboard on the player's device.

3. Local Leaderboard Data

The local leaderboard may store gameplay scores and the optional name entered by the player. This information is stored locally on the player's device for in-game display. It is not transmitted to Caffeine Studios, published online, or shared with other players.

Players can avoid entering a personal name by leaving the name field blank or using a nickname. Local leaderboard information may be removed by clearing the app data, resetting the game data if that option is available, or uninstalling the game.

4. Device Access and Permissions

Archery Target uses standard Meta Quest headset and controller input so the player can aim, shoot, and interact with the VR archery gameplay. The game does not request microphone access, voice chat access, camera access, contacts, precise location, or multiplayer communication features.

Any platform-level processing required for the Meta Quest headset, controllers, store, operating system, or device runtime is handled by Meta under Meta's own terms and privacy practices.

5. How We Use Information

Because Archery Target does not collect personal information, Caffeine Studios does not use player data for analytics, advertising, profiling, remarketing, or user tracking.

Local leaderboard information is used only to show scores and optional names inside the game on the same device.

6. Sharing Information

Caffeine Studios does not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information from Archery Target. The game has no multiplayer leaderboard, no online leaderboard, no social sharing system, and no in-game voice or chat feature.

7. Children's Privacy

Archery Target does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the game does not include accounts, multiplayer communication, microphone use, or online data collection by Caffeine Studios, no child personal information is requested by the game.

8. Contacting Us

If a player contacts Caffeine Studios outside the game for support, questions, or business communication, any information provided in that message will be used only to respond to the request.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Archery Target changes, if new features are added, or if legal or platform requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions about Archery Target, contact Caffeine Studios through the contact options listed on the Caffeine Studios website.
