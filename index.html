FAQ

Common questions from clients planning a Unity project.

Can you build just a prototype or MVP instead of a full game?

Yes. Many projects start with a focused prototype or MVP to validate mechanics, investor demos, user feedback, or market demand before a larger production commitment.

Which platforms can you support?

We support Unity projects for Android, iOS, Windows, Mac, WebGL, WebXR, Meta Quest VR, mobile AR, VR AR experiences, and Android TV depending on the project scope.

Do you handle multiplayer projects?

Yes. Multiplayer can be included as part of a broader development scope, with planning based on gameplay requirements, session flow, networking approach, and launch priorities.

How long does development usually take?

Timelines depend on complexity, content, and target platforms. A prototype may take weeks, while a polished MVP or complete production build may take months. We scope this clearly before work begins.

Do clients receive the source code and project files?

For custom development engagements, source code ownership and delivery expectations can be defined clearly in the project agreement so there is no ambiguity.

How do you communicate during development?

We keep communication straightforward through regular updates, milestone reviews, and direct channels such as Fiverr, Upwork, WhatsApp, Discord, or scheduled calls depending on the engagement.
