Privacy Policy

Dream Canvas VR: Drawing Studio

Dream Canvas VR: Drawing Studio is a single-player VR art experience for Meta Quest headsets and controllers. This policy explains how drawings and related information are handled by the game and what is not collected by Caffeine Studios.

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Quick Summary

  • No Caffeine Studios account or in-game login is required.
  • Caffeine Studios does not collect personal information through the game.
  • The game does not access Meta profile, user ID, age-group, or online-leaderboard data.
  • No advertising, in-app purchases, analytics, multiplayer, microphone, or camera features are used.
  • Drawings, save details, and thumbnails are stored locally on the player's device.
  • Saving also exports a PNG copy to the device's Pictures folder; Caffeine Studios does not upload it.

1. Who We Are

Dream Canvas VR: Drawing Studio is developed and published by Caffeine Studios. It is a single-player VR drawing studio where players create artwork with virtual tools and can save, reopen, and export their drawings.

2. Information We Collect

Caffeine Studios does not collect personal information through Dream Canvas VR: Drawing Studio. The game does not require a Caffeine Studios account, email address, phone number, player profile, username, or online login to play.

The game does not access the player's Meta Horizon username or display name, Meta Horizon User ID, user age group, friends list, or online leaderboard information.

Dream Canvas VR: Drawing Studio does not include Caffeine Studios advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, online accounts, online leaderboards, multiplayer, voice chat, or social-sharing services.

3. Local Drawing and Save Data

When a player saves artwork, the game stores drawing data locally on the device. This may include stroke positions, stroke widths, colors, an automatically generated save identifier and display name, creation and modification timestamps, and a thumbnail preview.

This local data is used to list, preview, reopen, and update drawings inside the game. It is not transmitted to Caffeine Studios, linked to a Caffeine Studios or Meta account, or shared with other players.

Local in-game saves may be removed by clearing the app data or uninstalling the game.

4. PNG Exports and Device Pictures

Saving a drawing also creates or updates a PNG image in the device's shared Pictures/Dream Canvas VR folder. This lets the player view the exported artwork in compatible device photo or gallery apps.

Caffeine Studios does not upload, receive, publish, or automatically share exported images. Other apps that the player authorizes to access device pictures may be able to access those images under their own terms and privacy practices.

Clearing app data or uninstalling the game may not remove PNG files already exported to the shared Pictures folder. Players can remove those copies through a compatible device photo, gallery, or file-management app.

5. Device Access and Permissions

Dream Canvas VR: Drawing Studio uses standard Meta Quest headset and controller input so the player can look around, draw, erase, select colors, interact with the studio, and use the save gallery. The game also writes player-created PNG images to shared device Pictures storage when artwork is saved.

The game does not request microphone access, voice chat access, camera access, contacts, precise location, or multiplayer communication features.

Any platform-level processing required for the Meta Quest headset, controllers, store, operating system, device runtime, or shared Pictures storage is handled by Meta under Meta's own terms and privacy practices.

6. How We Use Information

Because Dream Canvas VR: Drawing Studio does not collect personal information, Caffeine Studios does not use player data or artwork for analytics, advertising, profiling, remarketing, user tracking, artificial-intelligence training, or selling data.

Local drawing information is used only to provide the game's drawing, saving, thumbnail, gallery, reopening, and PNG-export features on the player's device.

7. Sharing Information and Artwork

Caffeine Studios does not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information or artwork from Dream Canvas VR: Drawing Studio. The game has no online gallery, multiplayer leaderboard, social posting system, or in-game voice or chat feature.

If a player chooses to share an exported PNG through Meta, another app, or another service outside the game, that user-directed sharing is controlled by the player and is subject to the selected service's privacy practices.

8. Children's Privacy

Dream Canvas VR: Drawing Studio does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the game does not include accounts, multiplayer communication, microphone use, or online data collection by Caffeine Studios, no child personal information is requested by the game.

9. Contacting Us

If a player contacts Caffeine Studios outside the game for support, questions, or business communication, any information provided in that message will be used only to respond to the request.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Dream Canvas VR: Drawing Studio changes, if new features are added, or if legal or platform requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions about Dream Canvas VR: Drawing Studio, contact Caffeine Studios through the contact options listed on the Caffeine Studios website.
