Privacy Policy

Traffic Moto VR: Bike Racing

Traffic Moto VR: Bike Racing is a single-player VR motorcycle racing game for Meta Quest-compatible headsets and controllers. This policy explains what information is handled by the game and what is not collected by Caffeine Studios.

Last updated: June 26, 2026

Quick Summary

  • No account is required.
  • No multiplayer features are included.
  • No microphone, voice chat, camera, or location access is used.
  • No advertising, in-app purchases, or analytics are used by Caffeine Studios.
  • Game progress, coins, unlocked content, and preferences are stored locally on the player's device.

1. Who We Are

Traffic Moto VR: Bike Racing is developed and published by Caffeine Studios. The game is a VR motorcycle racing experience where players use headset and controller input to ride, steer, brake, boost, select bikes, choose racing modes, and play through traffic racing challenges.

2. Information We Collect

Caffeine Studios does not collect personal information through Traffic Moto VR: Bike Racing. The game does not require an account, email address, phone number, profile, online login, or Caffeine Studios user account to play.

Traffic Moto VR: Bike Racing does not include Caffeine Studios advertising, analytics, real-money in-app purchases, online accounts, online leaderboards, multiplayer, voice chat, or social-sharing features.

3. Local Game Data

Traffic Moto VR: Bike Racing may store local game data such as coins, selected bike, unlocked bikes, unlocked levels, unlocked drive modes, selected level, selected racing mode, first-run status, rate-us prompt state, and gameplay progress on the player's device.

This local game data is used only to restore the player's game state and options between play sessions. It is not transmitted to Caffeine Studios, published online, or shared with other players.

4. Local Settings

The game may store local settings such as music volume, sound effects volume, engine volume, steering speed, steering mode, throttle mode, vibration preference, quality setting, visual effect setting, resolution setting, and related gameplay preferences on the player's device.

These settings are used only to apply the player's selected preferences on the same device. Local game data and settings may be removed by clearing the app data, using any in-game reset option if available, or uninstalling the game.

5. Device Access and Permissions

Traffic Moto VR: Bike Racing uses standard VR headset and controller input so the player can look around, steer, ride, brake, boost, honk, select menu items, and interact with the VR racing experience. The game does not request microphone access, voice chat access, camera access, contacts, precise location, or multiplayer communication features.

Any platform-level processing required for the Meta Quest headset, controllers, store, operating system, or device runtime is handled by Meta under Meta's own terms and privacy practices.

6. External Links

The game may include buttons such as Rate Us or More Games that open a Meta store page, Caffeine Studios profile, or other external store page outside the game. When a player opens an external page, the player's use of that page is governed by the relevant platform or website's own terms and privacy policy.

Caffeine Studios does not receive personal information from these local button clicks inside Traffic Moto VR: Bike Racing.

7. How We Use Information

Because Traffic Moto VR: Bike Racing does not collect personal information, Caffeine Studios does not use player data for analytics, advertising, profiling, remarketing, user tracking, or selling data.

Local game data and settings are used only to run the game, remember progress, and apply player preferences on the same device.

8. Sharing Information

Caffeine Studios does not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information from Traffic Moto VR: Bike Racing. The game has no multiplayer leaderboard, no online leaderboard, no social posting system, and no in-game voice or chat feature.

9. Children's Privacy

Traffic Moto VR: Bike Racing does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the game does not include accounts, multiplayer communication, microphone use, or online data collection by Caffeine Studios, no child personal information is requested by the game.

10. Contacting Us

If a player contacts Caffeine Studios outside the game for support, questions, or business communication, any information provided in that message will be used only to respond to the request.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Traffic Moto VR: Bike Racing changes, if new features are added, or if legal or platform requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions about Traffic Moto VR: Bike Racing, contact Caffeine Studios through the contact options listed on the Caffeine Studios website.
