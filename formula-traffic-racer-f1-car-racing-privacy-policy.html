Privacy Policy

Formula Traffic Racer: F1 Car Racing

Formula Traffic Racer: F1 Car Racing is developed by Caffeine Studios. The game operates locally on your Meta Quest headset and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Effective date: September 7, 2026

Quick Summary

  • No personal data is collected, transmitted, sold, or shared.
  • No account, advertising, analytics, tracking, backend service, or online leaderboard is used.
  • The best score is stored only on the device using Unity PlayerPrefs.
  • No camera, microphone, storage, location, contacts, or account access is requested.

1. Data Collection

Formula Traffic Racer: F1 Car Racing does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data. It does not ask for your name, email address, Meta account details, user ID, contacts, location, photos, recordings, payment information, analytics data, advertising data, or gameplay telemetry.

2. Local Game Data

The game saves the player's best score locally on the device using Unity PlayerPrefs. This data is used only to show and preserve the best result during gameplay. It is not uploaded, synchronized, or shared by the game.

3. Virtual-Reality Input

The game uses OpenXR headset and Touch controller tracking and input to provide seated cockpit driving, steering interaction, menus, collision feedback, and other virtual-reality gameplay. This input is processed in real time on the device and is not recorded, uploaded, or shared by the game.

4. Network Access, Advertising, and Analytics

Gameplay does not require an Internet connection. The game does not include advertisements, in-app purchases, online leaderboards, cloud saves, accounts, third-party analytics, crash reporting, or background network activity, and it does not send information to Caffeine Studios or any third party.

5. Device Access and Permissions

The game uses standard OpenXR capabilities required to run as a Meta Quest virtual-reality application. It does not request access to the camera, microphone, storage, location, contacts, or user accounts.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Local best-score data remains on your device until it is replaced through gameplay, cleared through the device's app-data controls, or removed by uninstalling the game. Because Caffeine Studios does not receive this data, there is no server-side player record for Caffeine Studios to access or delete.

7. Children's Privacy

Because the game does not collect or transmit personal data, it does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

8. Changes

If a future version adds any feature that changes these practices, this policy will be updated before that version is distributed.

9. Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to Caffeine Studios through its official support contact or store listing.
