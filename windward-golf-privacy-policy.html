Windward Golf Privacy Policy

Windward Golf is a single-player golf game for Meta Quest developed and published by Caffeine Studios. This policy explains how the game handles information, which services are involved, and how to contact us about privacy.

Effective and last updated November 19, 2026

1. Round saves, statistics, and settings

Windward Golf saves gameplay information locally on your headset so you can continue a round, view personal statistics, and keep your preferences. This includes:

  • Your current hole and round progress, strokes and penalties, hole scores, and the ball's position within the virtual course.
  • Completed rounds and holes, total shots and penalties, best and average scores, best hole, and longest shot. Random round identifiers and recent progress records help prevent duplicate statistics.
  • Your playing hand, distance units, ball trail preference, audio levels, and tutorial progress.

The Profile screen shows statistics saved on your headset; it is not an online account. The game does not upload these saves or statistics to Caffeine Studios or publish them on online leaderboards. Windward Golf has no Caffeine Studios account, multiplayer communication, in-game advertising, or separate gameplay analytics service operated by us.

2. VR input and device permissions

During play, Windward Golf processes headset and controller positions and orientations, controller buttons, and the tracking origin supplied by the VR runtime. These inputs render your view, operate menus, position your stance, and calculate club swings on your device. The virtual ball positions in a round save describe locations in the game, not your geographic location.

The game does not record or upload raw headset, controller, or eye-tracking data to Caffeine Studios. Gaze is not used to control golf. Supported tracking and rendering features are managed by the headset runtime and its permissions. The game does not access microphone recordings, camera images, contacts, or precise geographic location.

3. Meta services and external links

When Meta Horizon Store ownership verification is enabled, Windward Golf uses the Meta Platform SDK to check whether your signed-in Meta account is entitled to use the game. The game uses the success or error result to manage access. It does not request your Meta username, email address, friends list, or profile for gameplay, or store a Meta user ID in your local golf profile. Caffeine Studios does not receive your account password or payment details through the game.

More Games and Rate Us open destinations in the Meta Store. Meta operates the account, store, headset, and associated platform services, and may process information such as device diagnostics and service usage independently. These services are covered by the Meta Privacy Policy and Supplemental Meta Platforms Technologies Privacy Policy.

If platform backup is available and enabled for your app data, Meta may back up eligible saves and settings. Caffeine Studios does not operate a separate cloud save service and cannot directly access or delete data held only in your Meta account.

4. Support information and sharing

If you email Caffeine Studios, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or diagnostic details you choose to send. We use that information to respond, troubleshoot, and handle your privacy request. Please share only what is needed and do not send passwords or payment details.

We do not sell or rent your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Support messages are processed through our email service provider as needed to handle correspondence. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect rights and safety.

5. Retention and security

Local gameplay data remains on your device until replaced or removed. Starting a New Game replaces the current round but does not erase your accumulated Profile statistics or preferences. Platform backups may remain separately until you remove them.

We keep support correspondence only as needed to handle the request, resolve related issues, and meet applicable legal obligations. We take reasonable measures to protect information we hold, but no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Delete your data and make a privacy request

To remove your local round saves, Profile statistics, and preferences, clear Windward Golf's app data using your headset's storage or app settings, or uninstall the game. To prevent old data from being restored, also remove any Windward Golf cloud backups for your devices through Meta Cloud Backups or Meta's backup settings. Backup availability and controls depend on the platform.

To request deletion of information you sent us, or to ask to access or correct it, email thecaffeinestudio@gmail.com with the subject Windward Golf Data Deletion Request or a description of your privacy request. Identify the information or correspondence concerned. We may ask for the minimum information needed to verify the request. Requests are free of charge, and we respond within applicable legal time limits.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to restrict or object to certain processing, receive a copy of your information, or complain to a data protection authority. Caffeine Studios cannot remotely inspect or erase saves held only on your headset; use the device and backup controls above for that data.

7. Children's privacy

Windward Golf does not ask players to submit their name, age, email address, or other contact information in the game. Parents and guardians should use the age eligibility requirements and parental controls provided by Meta. If you believe a child has sent personal information to Caffeine Studios, contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

8. This website and policy updates

GitHub Pages hosts this policy page. GitHub may process connection and usage information, including IP addresses and request details, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This policy page does not embed advertising, analytics scripts, or third-party fonts.

We may update this policy when the game, services, or privacy practices change. The latest policy will be posted here with a revised date.
