Privacy Policy

Red Light Green Light VR

Red Light Green Light VR is a VR game for Meta Quest headsets and controllers, developed by Caffeine Studios. This policy explains how we handle information for in-game identity, avatars, leaderboard features and locally saved progress. Feature availability may vary by game version and Meta platform availability.

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Quick Summary

  • Meta username/display name is used for your in-game name.
  • Meta user ID supports account-linked platform and leaderboard features.
  • Meta age group supports age-appropriate platform and leaderboard access; exact age is not requested.
  • Meta avatar information may be used to display your avatar where supported.
  • Public leaderboards may show your name, score and rank.
  • Profile preferences and career progress are saved locally.
  • We do not sell player information.

1. Who We Are

Caffeine Studios develops Red Light Green Light VR. For privacy questions or requests concerning information we control, contact caffeinesproduction@gmail.com. No separate Caffeine Studios account is required to play.

2. Meta Account Information

With access permitted by Meta, the game may receive your Meta Horizon username or display name and Meta user ID. We use your name to display your identity in the game, including the Welcome and Profile screens. Your user ID identifies the correct account for platform features and, where enabled, associates your scores with your leaderboard entry.

The game can cache your Meta display name locally so it can show your name between sessions or when a platform request is unavailable. A generic player label may be shown when a name is unavailable.

3. Age Information

Meta User Age Group means a broad age category provided by Meta, rather than your exact age or date of birth. Where required for platform and leaderboard access, we use this information to support age-appropriate access and applicable Meta requirements.

We do not display your age group to other players, include it in public leaderboard entries, or use it to calculate score or rank. It is not used for advertising, marketing or profiling outside the game. The game does not ask you to enter an exact age or date of birth.

4. Avatars and Optional Profile Choice

Where your Meta account avatar is supported, the game may access the avatar information and assets supplied by Meta to load and display your avatar in the game. Your user ID may be used to retrieve the correct avatar. Avatar services may process the requests needed to deliver those assets.

The game also supports bundled avatar appearances. You may optionally select Male or Female to choose a matching avatar appearance, or skip this choice. This preference and the last selected bundled avatar are saved locally. You can change or remove the preference in Profile. We do not use it to infer your age or for advertising.

5. Leaderboards and Visibility

Where online leaderboards are enabled, your Meta user ID associates your submitted gameplay score or result with your account. Meta Platform and Meta Leaderboards process entries and rankings. Your username or display name, score and rank may be visible to other players through leaderboard displays. Do not use a public display name that contains information you do not want others to see.

Account identifiers support the platform services; they are not presented as public leaderboard labels. Age group is not part of the public entry. Local records and rankings against computer-controlled opponents can operate without submitting results to an online leaderboard.

6. Local Game Data

The game stores preferences and progress on your device, including cached display name, optional avatar preference, tutorial completion, settings, best and recent results, lifetime match statistics, play time, distance, level, medal and progression points. This data is used to restore your profile, settings and progress between sessions.

Locally saved data is separate from any Meta-hosted online leaderboard records. Clearing app data or uninstalling the game can remove local saves; this does not by itself delete platform-hosted records. Device or platform backup features, if used, are subject to the relevant provider's controls.

7. VR Input and Platform Services

The game processes headset and controller position, orientation and input to support looking around, arm-swing movement, avatar animation and menu interaction. This input is used for gameplay. We do not use it for advertising or identification outside the game.

Meta provides the headset runtime, account, store and associated platform services. Meta may process technical and account information under its own Privacy Policy. Optional More Games or store links open external pages governed by those pages' privacy practices.

8. Use and Sharing of Information

We use the information described above to provide in-game identity, avatars, age-appropriate platform access, leaderboard features, saved progress and responses to support requests. We do not sell, rent or trade player personal information, or use Meta account, age-group or avatar information for targeted advertising.

Meta processes the information required for its account, avatar and leaderboard services. Public leaderboard information may be visible to other players as described above. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect users and the service. We do not disclose age group or optional gender preference to other players through leaderboard entries.

9. Retention and Security

We retain information under our control only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, unless a legal obligation requires longer retention. Local progress and preferences remain on your device until removed or replaced. Meta-hosted records are subject to Meta's service controls and retention practices.

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information we handle. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You can manage your Meta username, display name and avatar through Meta account settings, and change or remove the optional avatar preference in the game's Profile screen. Clear the game's app data to remove locally saved profile information and progress.

For access, correction or deletion of information under Caffeine Studios' control, email caffeinesproduction@gmail.com with the subject Red Light Green Light VR Data Deletion Request. Include the Meta username or display name used in the game and enough detail to identify the relevant record. Do not send your password or an identity document. We may request proportionate information to verify that the request concerns your account.

We will respond and use available tools to address records we control, in accordance with applicable law. Requests involving Meta-controlled account, age-category or avatar information may need to be made directly to Meta. If platform limitations or legal obligations prevent deletion, we will explain the limitation. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to object to or restrict processing, obtain a copy of your information, or contact your local data protection authority.

11. Children's Privacy

Players must meet the game's published age rating and applicable Meta account requirements. Where age-group information is available for platform access, it is used as described above. Parents or guardians can contact us about a child's information or request deletion of information under our control. If we learn that information was collected contrary to applicable children's privacy requirements, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

12. Support and Policy Updates

If you email us, we use the contact details and information you provide to respond to your request. Please share only what is needed for support or your privacy request.

We may update this policy when game features, data practices or legal and platform requirements change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised Last updated date. Contact caffeinesproduction@gmail.com for privacy questions.
