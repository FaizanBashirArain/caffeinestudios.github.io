Privacy Policy

Zombie Car VR: Crush & Survive

Zombie Car VR: Crush & Survive is a single-player VR zombie-driving survival game for Meta Quest headsets and controllers. This policy explains what information is handled by the game and what is not collected by Caffeine Studios.

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Quick Summary

  • No Caffeine Studios account or in-game login is required.
  • Caffeine Studios does not collect personal information through the game.
  • The game does not access Meta profile, user ID, age-group, or online-leaderboard data.
  • No multiplayer, microphone, voice chat, or camera features are included.
  • No advertising, in-app purchases, or analytics are used by Caffeine Studios.
  • A best-kill score may be stored locally on the player's device.

1. Who We Are

Zombie Car VR: Crush & Survive is developed and published by Caffeine Studios. The game is a single-player VR survival experience where players drive a vehicle, crush zombies, and try to survive for as long as possible.

2. Information We Collect

Caffeine Studios does not collect personal information through Zombie Car VR: Crush & Survive. The game does not require a Caffeine Studios account, email address, phone number, player profile, or in-game online login to play.

The game does not access the player's Meta Horizon username or display name, Meta Horizon User ID, user age group, friends list, or online leaderboard information.

Zombie Car VR: Crush & Survive does not include Caffeine Studios advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, online accounts, online leaderboards, multiplayer, voice chat, or social-sharing features.

3. Local Game Data

The game may save the player's best-kill score locally on the player's device so it can be displayed in future play sessions. This score is gameplay data and is not linked to a name, account, Meta profile, or other personal information.

Local game data is not transmitted to Caffeine Studios, published online, or shared with other players. It may be removed by clearing the app data or uninstalling the game.

4. Device Access and Permissions

Zombie Car VR: Crush & Survive uses standard Meta Quest headset and controller input so the player can look around, drive, steer, interact, and play in the VR environment. The game does not request microphone access, voice chat access, camera access, contacts, precise location, or multiplayer communication features.

Any platform-level processing required for the Meta Quest headset, controllers, store, operating system, or device runtime is handled by Meta under Meta's own terms and privacy practices.

5. How We Use Information

Because Zombie Car VR: Crush & Survive does not collect personal information, Caffeine Studios does not use player data for analytics, advertising, profiling, remarketing, user tracking, or selling data.

The locally saved best-kill score is used only to show the player's best result inside the game on the same device.

6. Sharing Information

Caffeine Studios does not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information from Zombie Car VR: Crush & Survive. The game has no multiplayer leaderboard, no online leaderboard, no social posting system, and no in-game voice or chat feature.

7. Children's Privacy

Zombie Car VR: Crush & Survive does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the game does not include accounts, multiplayer communication, microphone use, or online data collection by Caffeine Studios, no child personal information is requested by the game.

8. Contacting Us

If a player contacts Caffeine Studios outside the game for support, questions, or business communication, any information provided in that message will be used only to respond to the request.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Zombie Car VR: Crush & Survive changes, if new features are added, or if legal or platform requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions about Zombie Car VR: Crush & Survive, contact Caffeine Studios through the contact options listed on the Caffeine Studios website.
