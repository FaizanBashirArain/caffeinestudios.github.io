Privacy Policy

Stuntline VR: Car Parkour

Stuntline VR: Car Parkour is a VR car parkour game for Meta Quest-compatible headsets and controllers. This policy explains what information is handled by the game, including the username used for the global leaderboard.

Last updated: July 9, 2026

Quick Summary

  • No Caffeine Studios account is required.
  • A player-entered username is used for the global leaderboard.
  • Leaderboard scores and related gameplay results may be submitted online.
  • No microphone, voice chat, camera, contacts, or precise location access is used.
  • Caffeine Studios does not sell player information.

1. Who We Are

Stuntline VR: Car Parkour is developed and published by Caffeine Studios. The game is a VR driving and parkour experience where players use headset and controller input to drive cars, complete stunt challenges, and compete for scores on a global leaderboard.

2. Information We Collect

Stuntline VR: Car Parkour does not require a Caffeine Studios account, email address, phone number, real name, profile, online login, or social media account to play.

The game uses a player-entered username for the global leaderboard. This username may be submitted with leaderboard scores, level results, completion times, ranking data, or related gameplay performance information so other players can see leaderboard entries.

Players should not enter a real name or other personal information as their leaderboard username. A nickname or non-identifying username is recommended.

3. Global Leaderboard Data

When a player uses the global leaderboard, the game may send the player's chosen username, score, rank, level or challenge identifier, completion result, and related gameplay statistics needed to display and maintain leaderboard standings.

Leaderboard entries are used to show player rankings inside the game and may be visible to other players. Caffeine Studios uses this information only to operate, display, maintain, and protect the leaderboard feature.

Platform services, leaderboard providers, hosting providers, or network infrastructure used to operate the leaderboard may process technical information needed to transmit, store, and protect leaderboard data, such as device/network connection data, timestamps, request logs, or service identifiers.

4. Local Game Data and Settings

Stuntline VR: Car Parkour may store local game data such as player progress, unlocked content, selected car, challenge status, best local results, preferences, and gameplay settings on the player's device.

Local data and settings are used to restore the player's game state and preferences between play sessions. Local data may be removed by clearing the app data, using any in-game reset option if available, or uninstalling the game.

5. Device Access and Permissions

Stuntline VR: Car Parkour uses standard VR headset and controller input so the player can look around, drive, navigate menus, and interact with the VR parkour experience. The game does not request microphone access, voice chat access, camera access, contacts, precise location, or in-game chat features from Caffeine Studios.

Any platform-level processing required for the Meta Quest headset, controllers, store, operating system, device runtime, or platform services is handled by Meta under Meta's own terms and privacy practices.

6. External Links and Platform Services

The game may include buttons such as Rate Us, More Games, or store-related links that open a Meta store page, Caffeine Studios profile, or other external page outside the game. When a player opens an external page, the player's use of that page is governed by the relevant platform or website's own terms and privacy policy.

Online leaderboard functionality may rely on third-party platform, hosting, or backend services. Those services may process leaderboard and technical service data as needed to provide the leaderboard feature.

7. How We Use Information

Caffeine Studios uses leaderboard information only to operate Stuntline VR: Car Parkour's global leaderboard, show rankings, preserve score history, prevent misuse, diagnose technical issues, and improve the reliability of the game experience.

Caffeine Studios does not use leaderboard usernames or scores to sell data, target advertising, profile players outside the game, or contact players.

8. Sharing Information

Caffeine Studios does not sell, rent, or trade player personal information. Leaderboard usernames and score entries may be shown to other players as part of the global leaderboard.

Leaderboard and technical service data may be processed by service providers that help operate the leaderboard, hosting, storage, security, or platform functionality. Caffeine Studios uses these providers only to support game features and related service operations.

9. Player Choices

Players can reduce personal information in leaderboard entries by choosing a nickname that does not identify them. If the game offers an option to change the username, reset data, or skip leaderboard submission, players can use those options inside the game.

For privacy questions or leaderboard-related requests, players can contact Caffeine Studios through the contact options listed on the Caffeine Studios website.

10. Children's Privacy

Stuntline VR: Car Parkour does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Players should not enter a real name, email address, phone number, school name, location, or other personal information as a leaderboard username.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has submitted personal information through a leaderboard username, they may contact Caffeine Studios and request assistance.

11. Contacting Us

If a player contacts Caffeine Studios outside the game for support, privacy questions, leaderboard requests, or business communication, any information provided in that message will be used only to respond to the request.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Stuntline VR: Car Parkour changes, if leaderboard features change, if new features are added, or if legal or platform requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions about Stuntline VR: Car Parkour, contact Caffeine Studios through the contact options listed on the Caffeine Studios website.
