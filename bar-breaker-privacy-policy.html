Privacy Policy

Bar Breaker

Bar Breaker is a single-player VR game for Meta Quest-compatible headsets and controllers. This policy explains what information is handled by the game and what is not collected by Caffeine Studios.

Last updated: June 15, 2026

Quick Summary

  • No account is required.
  • No multiplayer features are included.
  • No microphone, voice chat, or camera access is used.
  • No advertising, in-app purchases, or analytics are used by Caffeine Studios.
  • Audio volume preferences may be stored locally on the player's device.

1. Who We Are

Bar Breaker is developed and published by Caffeine Studios. The game is a VR bar destruction experience where players use headset and controller input to move, interact with objects, break items, and play inside the VR environment.

2. Information We Collect

Caffeine Studios does not collect personal information through Bar Breaker. The game does not require an account, email address, phone number, profile, online login, or Caffeine Studios user account to play.

Bar Breaker does not include Caffeine Studios advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, online accounts, online leaderboards, multiplayer, voice chat, or social-sharing features.

3. Local Game Settings

Bar Breaker may store local gameplay preferences such as background music volume and sound effects volume on the player's device. These preferences are used only to keep the selected audio settings between play sessions.

Local settings are not transmitted to Caffeine Studios, published online, or shared with other players. They may be removed by clearing the app data or uninstalling the game.

4. Device Access and Permissions

Bar Breaker uses standard VR headset and controller input so the player can look around, move, grab, aim, break objects, and interact with the VR scene. The game does not request microphone access, voice chat access, camera access, contacts, precise location, or multiplayer communication features.

Any platform-level processing required for the Meta Quest headset, controllers, store, operating system, or device runtime is handled by Meta under Meta's own terms and privacy practices.

5. How We Use Information

Because Bar Breaker does not collect personal information, Caffeine Studios does not use player data for analytics, advertising, profiling, remarketing, user tracking, or selling data.

Local audio settings are used only to apply the player's selected sound preferences on the same device.

6. Sharing Information

Caffeine Studios does not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information from Bar Breaker. The game has no multiplayer leaderboard, no online leaderboard, no social posting system, and no in-game voice or chat feature.

7. Children's Privacy

Bar Breaker does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the game does not include accounts, multiplayer communication, microphone use, or online data collection by Caffeine Studios, no child personal information is requested by the game.

8. Contacting Us

If a player contacts Caffeine Studios outside the game for support, questions, or business communication, any information provided in that message will be used only to respond to the request.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Bar Breaker changes, if new features are added, or if legal or platform requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions about Bar Breaker, contact Caffeine Studios through the contact options listed on the Caffeine Studios website.
