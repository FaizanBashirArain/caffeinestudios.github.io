FAQ

Before we start.

Can you build a complete Meta Quest game?

Yes. I can take a focused VR concept through prototyping, gameplay development, content integration, UI, optimization, Quest builds, testing support, and store-preparation deliverables.

Can you join or repair an existing Unity VR project?

Yes. I can diagnose a current project, fix interactions or build issues, complete features, improve performance, and work carefully within an established architecture.

Do you work with passthrough and mixed reality?

Yes. Darts VR: Real Room is one example of a shipped passthrough product. The right implementation depends on the gameplay goal and the Meta Quest features your project actually needs.
